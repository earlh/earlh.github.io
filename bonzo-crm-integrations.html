Bonzo CRM: API Integrations, Automation & Lead Routing

What is Bonzo?

Bonzo is a mortgage CRM and SMS/engagement platform built specifically for mortgage loan officers and lending teams. It's broadly similar to GoHighLevel, but purpose-built for loan officers. Teams use it to text, call, and nurture leads.

Who am I?

I'm a multi-time startup founder ( DataGrail , YC), and I build Bonzo integrations.

What I've built / can help with

Things I've built directly against the Bonzo API:

  • Data ingest
    • Batch lead insertion, with lead scrubbing and deduplication before contacts are created in Bonzo
    • Webhook listeners (Freerate, Lendgo, Leadpoint, LendingTree, etc) to push individual leads to Bonzo
  • Enrichment and routing
    • Enrichment (appending and normalizing lead data) prior to routing
    • Rules-based assignment/routing of leads to specific loan officers
  • Data synchronization
    • Webhook handling for inbound SMS and status changes
    • Sync logic to keep external systems and Bonzo contacts consistent at scale
    • Two-way sync between Bonzo and Calltools
    • Zapier and Lovable listeners (to and from)
  • Image quoting — custom mortgage quotes, individualized to each lead, sent as SMS

A Clay-style layer for Bonzo

If you're familiar with Clay -style data enrichment and routing workflows, that's the closest analogy. The main system I've built sits in front of Bonzo: leads come in, get scrubbed and deduplicated, enriched with the data needed to prioritize and route them, and get assigned to a specific loan officer. Bonzo runs the CRM and SMS side; this layer is what decides which lead a loan officer sees, when, and with what context.
